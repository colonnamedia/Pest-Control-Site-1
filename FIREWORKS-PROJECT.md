# FireWorks Project — Pest Patrol

## Template family

Recurring Property Services. Primary demo industry: pest control. Adaptable to residential/commercial cleaning, lawn care, pool maintenance, and general property maintenance.

## Demo safety

Pest Patrol is a fictional demonstration identity. All phone numbers, emails, service claims, plan details, locations, credentials, availability, and operational language must be replaced and verified before client launch. Forms validate locally only and do not submit or store information.

## Included pages

- Home
- Services hub
- General pest control
- Termite control
- Rodent control
- Protection plans
- Commercial services
- Service areas
- Request an inspection

## Reusable sections

- Hero with inspection-first CTA
- Quick pest/property/ZIP qualifier
- Residential and commercial service cards
- Recurring plan comparison
- Four-step service process
- Service-area hub
- Commercial facility modules
- Full inspection intake form
- Demo disclaimers and safe form success state

## Primary CTA and intake fields

Primary CTA: Request an inspection.

Fields: name, business name, email, phone, property type, pest/concern, property ZIP, preferred contact, detailed observations, recurring-plan interest, and demo acknowledgment.

- Basic booking / appointment integration: $199 setup (FireWorks storefront add-on); provider confirmed per client

## Required client inputs

- Legal business name, logo, colors, and verified contact information
- Accurate hours and service territory
- Actual services, covered pests, exclusions, and preparation instructions
- Real plan names, prices, visit cadence, cancellation terms, and guarantees
- Verified licenses, insurance, certifications, and credentials
- Real reviews with permission
- Scheduling/dispatch/CRM provider and credentials
- Privacy, consent, and data-retention requirements
- Original photos or approved stock-image direction
